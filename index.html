One room.

Questions

Do I need any experience?

None at all. Most guests come for the first time without knowing what to expect.

What should I wear?

Comfortable clothes. You stay dressed for every session.

Is this a medical treatment?

No. Energy healing is a relaxation practice and does not diagnose or treat any condition. Please keep seeing your doctor for any health concerns.
